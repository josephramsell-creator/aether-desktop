Aether 2.0.0 — replace v1
=========================

1. Close Aether.
2. Unzip Aether-Windows-x64.zip to a new folder (recommended)
   or overwrite your existing Aether folder.
3. If you already edited readings, copy your old
   data/content/aether-production.xlsx into the new folder
   after unzipping.
4. Run Aether.exe.

What is new
- Production workbook (date, sign, reading, music, voice, font)
- Edit the reading and Save to workbook
- Medallion, layout, and typewriter are unchanged
